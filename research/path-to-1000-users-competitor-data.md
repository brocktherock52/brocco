## patterns

### pricing
Dominant first paid tier sits at **$19-$20/mo** (Cursor, Devin, Replit, Relevance, n8n Starter). **$49.99 (Lindy) is the outlier on the high side** for a sticker first tier, and Lindy is the only one with no free tier. CrewAI has no published self-serve price.

**Conclusion**: $20 is the gravity well for individual-developer agentic SaaS. $49 only works if you skip free tier and target operators willing to pay for assistant-style productivity (Lindy's playbook).

### acquisition channels (recurring across the cohort)
1. **Open-source GitHub funnel** — n8n and CrewAI both explicitly credit this. Stars → deploys → paid feature requests.
2. **Founder-led X/Twitter** — Lindy's first 70k waitlist came from a single demo video. Amjad Masad runs Replit's X presence personally.
3. **Viral launch demo on X** — Cognition's March 2024 "Devin" launch video drove the inbound wave that took them from $0 to $1M ARR by Sept 2024.
4. **Engineer word-of-mouth + dogfooding** — Cursor's stated takeaway in Lenny's deep dive.
5. **Influencer/YouTuber moment** — Lindy's MattVidPro inflection point.
6. **Bottoms-up enterprise inbound from OSS** — CrewAI: Oracle phoned them.

Notably absent or unstated in fetched sources: YC demo day, Hacker News, comparison SEO, model-provider partnerships. None of these companies publicly credited those as primary in the material I pulled.

### free-to-paid conversion benchmarks (dev-tools / agentic SaaS)
From [ChartMogul's 2026 SaaS Conversion Report](https://chartmogul.com/reports/saas-conversion-report/), [First Page Sage 2026](https://firstpagesage.com/seo-blog/saas-freemium-conversion-rates/), [Guru Startups](https://www.gurustartups.com/reports/freemium-to-paid-conversion-rate-benchmarks):
- **Freemium self-serve: 3-5% good, 6-12% great**
- **Opt-in free trial: ~8.9% average**
- **CC-required free trial: ~31.4% average** (Lindy's model)
- Sub-5-min time-to-value drives 13-16% visitor-to-signup on freemium

---

## five takeaways

1. **Price the entry tier at $20, not $49**, unless you're going CC-required-trial like Lindy. Every breakout in the cohort except Lindy anchored at $19-$20.
2. **Open source is the cheapest agentic-SaaS distribution channel in the cohort** — n8n and CrewAI are the only two who hit thousands of paying customers without burning paid acquisition, and both credit GitHub explicitly.
3. **One viral demo can do the work of a year of marketing** — Devin's launch video, Lindy's March 2023 demo (70k waitlist), MattVidPro's Lindy 2.0 video. Cold start + 1 high-quality demo + founder-led X = the modal path.
4. **Free-to-paid conversion math for 1,000 paying users**: at the cohort-realistic 4% freemium conversion, you need ~25,000 active free signups; at 9% trial conversion, ~11,000 trial starts; at 31% CC-trial conversion (Lindy model), ~3,200 trial starts. **The Lindy model is 8x more capital-efficient on top-of-funnel, which is why $49.99 with no free tier works.**
5. **Time to 1k paying customers correlates with launch-event quality, not feature breadth** — Cognition got there in months on a single video; n8n took years on slow OSS compounding. For a BYOK Claude $49/mo SaaS launching cold today, the dominant strategy in this dataset is: ship an OSS or free-deployable core, do a founder-led launch video on X, and reserve the $49 tier for a CC-required trial flow.
