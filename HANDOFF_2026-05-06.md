## design language reference (locked)

- **Palette**: bg #050807, mid #0a1116, accent cyan #67E8F9,
  brand violet #7C3AED, lavender #C4B5FD, plus per-agent accents
- **Type stack**: Inter (sans), JetBrains Mono (mono for eyebrows +
  agent/tool names + metric numbers), Newsreader/Instrument Serif
  italic (accent words inside H1/H2)
- **Headline gradient**: white → #E9EEF1 → #C4B5FD
- **Brand gradient**: #C4B5FD → #67E8F9 (lavender → cyan)
- **Eyebrows**: mono, 10.5px, uppercase, tracking 0.18em, faint
- **Pills**: rounded-full, 1px white/8% border, white/2% bg, mono uppercase
- **Cards**: rounded-2xl, white/6% border, white/2% bg, backdrop-blur
- **Buttons**: btn-primary (cyan-violet gradient), btn-ghost (white/4%)
- **Container**: max 1200px, px-5 md:px-8
- **No em-dashes anywhere** in user-facing copy
- **No AI vocabulary** in copy: delve / leverage / robust / journey /
  seamless / unleash / elevate
