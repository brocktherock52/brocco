Brock dislikes the em-dash character (`—`, U+2014) in any deliverable he ships from this workspace.

**Why:** stylistic preference. Em-dashes also pattern-match as "AI-written" copy in 2026 and undercut the brand voice he wants for brocco.ai and other arms.

**How to apply:**
- Never use `—` in marketing copy, blog posts, READMEs, code comments, Slack/email drafts, or any user-visible deliverable.
- Replace with: a period (`.`), comma (`,`), colon (`:`), parenthetical `(...)`, or hyphen-with-spaces (` - `) depending on what reads cleanest.
- After writing any deliverable, grep for `—` and substitute before declaring done.
- Applies to both en-dashes (`–`) and em-dashes (`—`); prefer plain hyphens or rephrasing.
- Conversational chat with Brock: also avoid them.
