When Brock points at an outside source (Schmidt LinkedIn post, a competitor, a tweet, a video) as the spark for a pivot, treat the source as inspiration, not as the headline.

**Why:** users came up under prior session where I wove "Schmidt's thesis" through every section of brocco.ai marketing, including the hero quote. Brock pushed back: "that was just the inspiration." Constantly citing the inspiration source makes the brand feel derivative and dates the marketing the moment that source goes stale.

**How to apply:**
- Use the framing/thesis as substance in the deliverable, but rephrase it as the brand's own voice.
- One quiet attribution at most (e.g. a small "inspired by..." link in a footer or about page), not as a recurring section.
- Strip names of the inspiration source from headlines, hero copy, blockquotes that anchor a section, and repeated mentions.
- Keep the underlying insight; lose the celebrity name.
